Sources and evidence

Every piece of advice in Toilet Pup and on this site is paraphrased from the sources below. Where something is common trainer practice rather than published guidance, we say so. Strength: Study is peer-reviewed research, Vet is veterinary clinical guidance, Welfare is animal welfare charity guidance, Practice is common trainer practice.

How predictions work

Predictions start from welfare guidance on how often puppies of each age need to go, with an upper limit from the widely used "age in months plus one hours" rule of thumb. They adjust for waking, meals, drinks and play. With Pro, a statistical model learns from your own puppy's logs, giving more weight to recent days because bladder control improves week by week. Predictions are always shown as a time window, because they're estimates and every puppy is different.
