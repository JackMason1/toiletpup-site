Toilet Pup

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Short version: Toilet Pup does not collect any data. Everything you log stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your puppy's name, birth date, size, optional photo, and the events you log (wees, poos, meals, naps and so on). This is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage, so the app, its widgets and Siri shortcuts can use it.

What we collect

Nothing. Toilet Pup has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The developer cannot see your data. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You can change this in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Your control

You can export all your data as a CSV file, or delete everything, from the app's Settings screen. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Children

Toilet Pup does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
