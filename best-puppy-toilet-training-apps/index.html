Two kinds of app

Full training programmes like Zigzag and Dogo teach everything from sit to recall, with lessons and sometimes trainer chat. They're great if you want a whole course, and they're usually subscriptions.

Potty logs like Puddle, PupIQ, Doggy Time, Potty Whiz, pupdate and Toilet Pup focus on one job: tracking wees and poos so you know when to go out. Toilet training lasts a few months, so a one-off price often makes more sense than a subscription.

What to look for

  • Fast logging. You'll log 10 or more times a day, often holding a puppy. Widgets and Lock Screen buttons matter more than features you'll never open.
  • Naps and nights. Reminders that fire while your puppy is asleep are worse than useless.
  • Honest predictions. No app can know the exact minute. A time window, with a reason, is more trustworthy.
  • Total cost. Multiply any monthly price by the months you'll actually use it.
  • Good advice. Look for guidance that says where it comes from, and tells you when to see a vet.
