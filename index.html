Toilet Pup

The easiest way to toilet train your puppy.

Log a wee in one tap from your Lock Screen. See when they'll need to go next. Get a nudge before the accident, not after.

One tap, anywhere

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center, the Action Button, Siri and notification buttons. Log without opening the app.

Knows when they need to go

Predictions from the first hour, based on age and size, shown as an honest time window. With Pro it learns your puppy's own pattern.

Quiet while they sleep

Nap and bedtime modes pause reminders. No pings while your puppy is finally asleep.

Smart nudges

Just woke up? Just eaten? Toilet Pup knows the moments that matter and reminds you at the right time.
