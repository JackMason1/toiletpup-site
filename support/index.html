How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Toilet Pup and choose Quick Log. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, then tap the widget area under the time.

How do I log from Control Center or the Action Button?

On iOS 18 or later, open Control Center, tap the plus in the top corner, tap Add a Control and search for Toilet Pup. To use the Action Button, go to Settings, Action Button, choose Controls and pick Log Wee.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Try "Log a wee in Toilet Pup" or "Next toilet break in Toilet Pup".

How are predictions made?

From the first hour, Toilet Pup uses your puppy's age and size, based on guidance from UK welfare charities, and adjusts for moments like waking, eating and play. With Pro, it also learns from your puppy's own logs, giving more weight to recent days because bladder control improves week by week. It always shows a time window rather than an exact minute, because puppies are not clocks.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Reminders pause while your puppy is napping or in bed. Check that notifications are allowed for Toilet Pup in your iPhone's Settings, and that Break reminders is on in the app's Settings.

I bought Pro on another device

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Pro is also shared with your Family Sharing group.

Deleting your data

Settings, Delete all data removes every puppy and log from your device. You can export a CSV first.

Toilet Pup is a training aid, not veterinary advice. If accidents suddenly increase, or your puppy strains or goes very often, speak to your vet.
